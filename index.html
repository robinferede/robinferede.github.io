Robin Ferede

PhD Researcher · MAVLab, TU Delft

Faculty of Aerospace Engineering

TU Delft, Delft, NL

I’m a PhD candidate in Aerospace Engineering at TU Delft, working with the Micro Air Vehicle Laboratory (MAVLab) on time-optimal quadcopter control. My research sits at the intersection of optimal control, reinforcement learning, and world models, with a particular focus on closing the sim-to-real gap so that policies trained in simulation can fly real hardware at the limits of performance.

Most recently, I’ve been part of the TU Delft team at the A2RL Autonomous Drone Race in Abu Dhabi. In 2025 our MonoRace algorithm beat three FPV champions in the AI-vs-Human race and won both the Speed and Drag races. In 2026 we returned with SkyDreamer, a world-model-based pixel-to-action policy, and won the Multi-Drone Race.

Looking ahead, I’m interested in extending this work toward physical AI that generalizes across tasks and embodiments.
